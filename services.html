Health Awareness

Swine Flu (H1N1): Stay Alert, Stay Safe

Swine flu is a contagious viral illness. Its symptoms can look like a normal cold and fever, but timely care stops it from becoming serious.

Tap the poster to enlarge (Marathi)

Symptoms

  • Sudden fever
  • Headache
  • Cough and sore throat
  • Body ache and tiredness
  • Runny nose
  • Difficulty breathing

How to Stay Safe

  • Wash hands often with soap
  • Wear a mask
  • Avoid crowded places
  • Cover your mouth when coughing or sneezing
  • Keep your home and surroundings clean
  • Eat nutritious food and drink plenty of water

If you notice these symptoms, consult a doctor right away. Timely testing and treatment is the best protection.
