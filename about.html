About Us

Your Neighbourhood Lab in Moshi & Chikhali

Surya Pathology Laboratory has two branches: our main centre on River Road, Moshi, and a sub-centre on Chikhali-Akurdi Road, Chikhali. We test blood, urine and sputum samples and run a full range of hormone tests, so doctors and families can rely on our results. Both branches are open every day from 8 AM to 10 PM.
