FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide home sample collection?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 96077 21244 to book, and our collector will visit your home.

How will I receive my report?

We send reports on WhatsApp or email. You can also collect a printed copy from the lab.

Do I need to fast before a blood test?

Some tests, such as fasting sugar and lipid profile, need 10–12 hours of fasting. We'll tell you when you book.

Which hormone tests do you offer?

We offer all major hormone tests, including thyroid (T3, T4, TSH), fertility hormones, insulin and more.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Not for most routine tests. If you have a prescription, please share it so we run the right tests.
